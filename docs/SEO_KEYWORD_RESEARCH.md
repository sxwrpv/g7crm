# SEO keyword research: G7 Systems

## Method and limits

This is qualitative positioning research for the current public site. It intentionally includes no search-volume, traffic or ranking estimates because no verified keyword-platform data was supplied. Themes should be validated later with Search Console and a reputable keyword tool before prioritising new pages.

## Primary theme

**CRM and lead recovery for Irish home-service trades**

- Example queries: `CRM for trades Ireland`, `trade CRM Ireland`, `lead management for plumbers Ireland`, `lead recovery for Irish trades`.
- Intent: commercial investigation; a trade-business owner is comparing ways to capture, organise and follow up enquiries.
- Current-page mapping: title and description, hero positioning, five-stage workflow, G7CRM qualification stage, packages and audit CTA.

## Secondary themes

### Missed-call text-back

- Example queries: `missed call text back Ireland`, `automatic text reply for missed calls`, `missed call lead recovery for trades`.
- Intent: solution-aware commercial investigation.
- Current-page mapping: Capture and Respond workflow stages, missed-call foundation service and example scenario.

### WhatsApp Business automation

- Example queries: `WhatsApp Business automation Ireland`, `WhatsApp auto reply for trades`, `WhatsApp lead follow up`.
- Intent: setup/service comparison.
- Current-page mapping: hero, Capture and Respond stages, WhatsApp Business setup card and packages.

### Lead follow-up and booking workflow

- Example queries: `lead follow up for trades`, `quote follow up system`, `trade booking reminders`, `CRM follow up automation`.
- Intent: operational problem solving with commercial intent.
- Current-page mapping: Qualify and Book & Follow Up stages, package scope and audit.

### Google Business Profile setup

- Example queries: `Google Business Profile setup Ireland`, `Google Business Profile setup for trades`, `Google business setup for plumber`.
- Intent: local-visibility setup service.
- Current-page mapping: foundations/setup services only. Copy correctly describes setup for the customer's profile and does not claim that G7 has a profile or premises.

### Google review requests

- Example queries: `Google review request system`, `automated review requests for trades`, `get customer reviews Ireland`.
- Intent: reputation workflow/service investigation.
- Current-page mapping: Grow workflow stage, Google review system foundation and Growth package.

### Permission-aware customer reactivation

- Example queries: `customer reactivation email`, `email follow up for trade customers`, `past customer follow up Ireland`.
- Intent: retention and repeat-work workflow.
- Current-page mapping: Grow stage, customer email follow-up foundation and Growth package. Messaging should continue to avoid implying unrestricted marketing permission.

### Custom websites for trade businesses

- Example queries: `website design for trades`, `plumber website design Ireland`, `electrician website Ireland`, `trade website design Dublin`.
- Intent: service comparison and portfolio evaluation.
- Current-page mapping: custom business website foundation and clearly labelled fictional design examples.

## Search-intent coverage on the current page

- Problem awareness: missed calls, scattered inboxes, reminders and dormant customer relationships.
- Solution awareness: five-stage capture-to-grow workflow with explicit automation and human handoff.
- Commercial evaluation: foundations, monthly package scope, exact recurring prices and individually quoted setup fee.
- Conversion: free audit CTA and unchanged lead form.

## Measurement next steps

- Verify the canonical property in Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Use Search Console query/page data to refine title and copy after enough impressions accrue.
- Track audit-form submissions without recording sensitive form content in analytics.
- Review real customer language from audits and enquiries to improve terminology.
- Avoid ranking guarantees, fabricated case studies, invented review totals and unverified performance statistics.
